# Prompt Builder

最终图像编辑提示词按下面顺序拼装，避免 KV 创意覆盖 Food Fidelity。

## 1. Source Truth

Use the uploaded food photo as the authoritative source of truth for the exact real food subject.

## 2. Food Fidelity Locks

Preserve at least 95% of the original food identity and ingredient geometry, and at least 98% of the original vessel identity. Preserve visible ingredients, cuts, quantity relationships, stacking, plating, broth/sauce state, container material, shape and color. Do not redesign or re-plate the food.

## 3. Commercial Re-photography

First transform the source into premium cinematic commercial food photography through lighting, depth, exposure, background, material rendering and color grading. The food itself must remain conservative and highly faithful.

## 4. Dish Semantics

Dish: {{DISH_NAME}}
Cuisine / regional character: {{CUISINE}}
Flavor: {{FLAVOR}}
Semantic environment: {{SEMANTIC_SPACE}}

The background and art direction must belong to this dish, not a generic food template.

## 5. One Big Idea

Creative concept: {{ONE_BIG_IDEA}}

Build one strong visual concept only.

## 6. Hero Food

Place the exact preserved food as the nearest, largest and sharpest hero object. The food remains visual priority #1. Allow the vessel to approach or slightly exceed the frame when useful for impact.

## 7. Spatial Typography

Headline: {{HEADLINE}}
Mode: {{SPATIAL_TYPOGRAPHY_MODE}}

Turn the headline into a believable spatial element such as restaurant signage, an architectural frame, stage, wall, doorway or depth field appropriate to the cuisine. Keep the headline fully readable and exactly spelled.

## 8. Vanishing Point

Create one master vanishing point. Align the hero food perspective, table/surface, spatial headline, background architecture, signage, steam, lighting falloff and information modules into one coherent depth system.

## 9. Occlusion

Use controlled depth overlap: subtle steam, vessel rim or contextual elements may lightly cross headline edges, but never hide critical strokes. Keep occlusion minimal and physically believable.

## 10. Information System

Subtitle: {{SUBTITLE}}
Slogan / microcopy: {{SLOGAN}}
Selling points: {{SELLING_POINTS}}
Store / brand: {{STORE_NAME}}
Address: {{ADDRESS}}
Phone: {{PHONE}}

Create a complete but controlled restaurant campaign information system. Use 3–4 concise selling points when supported. Place business information in corners, side margins or perspective-compatible information zones. Do not create a heavy bottom bar.

If a real QR image is provided, preserve and place it in the reserved QR zone. Otherwise reserve a clean lower-right QR safe zone; do not invent a fake operational QR code.

## 12. Domain Firewall

Transfer campaign composition grammar only. Do not introduce HUD interfaces, cyberpunk neon, digital parameter cards, technology corridors, futuristic UI or consumer-electronics launch aesthetics unless explicitly requested.
